What It Does

Typed Contracts. Not Screenshots.

An Agentic Commerce storefront exposes your business's actions — checkAvailability, getCatalog, createReservation, getQuote — as typed WebMCP tools. AI agents call these tools directly with structured arguments and receive structured responses. No screenshot interpretation, no fuzzy chat, no fragile prompts.

We build the storefront, design the typed tools that match your business logic, and hand it over running on your domain. Chrome 146+, Claude Desktop, and emerging agentic browsers can transact on it.

WebMCP is a W3C standard (rolling out in Chrome 146+) that lets AI agents call typed tools on a website — like a webpage version of an API. Without it, AI agents have to read screenshots and pretend to click buttons, which is slow, fragile, and error-prone.
