What It Does

Your Business Data, Answered in Plain English.

An AI agent that turns your raw business data into answers. You type a question — "Which product had the highest return rate?" or "Show me revenue by region" — and the agent analyses your data and returns the answer.

Three tiers to match how your data is stored: as a file, in a connected platform, or in a database. Same question, different levels of flexibility.
