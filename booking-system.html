What It Does

Fully Automated Scheduling

An AI-powered booking system that handles scheduling, pricing, conflict detection, and revenue tracking — without a human in the loop. Customers describe what they need in plain language. The system finds the slot, confirms the price, and locks the booking.

It runs 24/7, handles midnight-crossing bookings, prevents double-bookings automatically, and gives you a live revenue dashboard.

How It Works

Three Steps to Full Automation

1

Your operating hours, resources (rooms, chairs, bays, courts), pricing tiers, and service durations.

2

You Track Everything

Daily bookings, revenue by slot and service type, peak hour patterns — all in your dashboard.
