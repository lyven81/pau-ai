What is an API Key

The Key Lives in Your Environment. Not Ours.

Your API key is yours entirely. When we set up your system, the key is stored in your own hosting environment — on your server, your cloud account, your .env file. Pau AI does not store, access, or retain your API key at any point, including during development and testing.

All requests from your system go directly to your chosen LLM provider (Anthropic, Google, OpenAI, etc.). We are never in the data path. If you ever want to revoke or rotate your key, you do it directly with your provider — no action required from us.
