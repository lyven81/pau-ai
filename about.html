The Founder

Lee Yih Ven — Data Analyst & AI Builder

I started Pau Analytics to help Malaysian SME owners make sense of their data. What I kept seeing was that the same owners also needed automation — not off-the-shelf tools, but systems built for how their specific business actually works. Pau AI is that answer.

I've built and deployed 38 AI applications across booking systems, analytics agents, research tools, and business chatbots — all production-deployed on Google Cloud, all built with real Malaysian business context. Every project on this site is a working reference app, not a mockup.

When you work with Pau AI, you're working with the person who built the system. That means direct communication, honest scoping, and accountability that stops with me — not a ticket queue.

Our Principles

Three Things We Never Compromise On

Built for Your Business, Not a Template

We configure every system to your actual workflow — your hours, your pricing rules, your service types, your language. The reference app is the starting point, not the finish line.

You Own Everything

The code is yours. The API key is yours. The hosting is yours. No lock-in, no monthly platform fee paid to us, no dependency on our continued involvement to keep your system running.

One Person Accountable

You work directly with the builder. When something needs fixing, you reach the same person who built it — not a support ticket queue.

Part of that ownership principle: your API key lives in your environment. We never touch it after handover.
