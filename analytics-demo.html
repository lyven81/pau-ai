Your Question
What is my revenue by day of week?
Generated Code
df['day'] = pd.to_datetime(df['date']).dt.day_name()
day_order = ['Monday','Tuesday','Wednesday','Thursday','Friday','Saturday','Sunday']
daily = df.groupby('day')['revenue'].sum().reindex(day_order)
daily.plot(kind='bar', color='#C9A84C')
AI Insight

Saturday and Sunday generate 38% of total weekly revenue despite being only 2 of 7 days. Wednesday is the weakest day at RM1,240 — 52% below the weekend average. A midweek promotion (e.g. Wednesday set meals) could meaningfully lift the weekly average.

Your Question
Show me monthly revenue trend for 2024
Generated Code
df['month'] = pd.to_datetime(df['date']).dt.to_period('M')
monthly = df.groupby('month')['revenue'].sum()
monthly.plot(kind='line', marker='o', color='#C9A84C', linewidth=2.5)
AI Insight

Revenue grew steadily from RM18,400 in January to a peak of RM31,200 in August, likely driven by school holidays. A notable dip in February (RM14,800) aligns with CNY — consider pre-CNY promotions next year. Q4 shows a secondary peak in December (RM28,600) worth capitalising on.

hourly = df.groupby('hour')['transaction_id'].count()

AI Insight

Two clear peaks emerge: morning rush (7–9am) and lunch peak (12–1pm), which together account for 58% of daily transactions. The 3–5pm window is significantly slower — consider afternoon promotions or reduced staffing during this window to optimise operating costs.
