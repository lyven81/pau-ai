What It Does

Plain Language In. Charts and Insights Out.

An AI agent that turns your raw business data into answers. You type a question in plain language — "Which product had the highest return rate last month?" or "Show me revenue by day of week" — and the agent generates the Python code, runs it safely, and returns a chart and insight.

No spreadsheet skills required. No waiting for a report. Just ask and get the answer.
