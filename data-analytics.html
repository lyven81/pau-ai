What It Does

MCP Tools Over Your Real Database. Not a Dashboard.

We connect an LLM (Claude / Gemini / your choice) to your real data via Model Context Protocol tools. The agent calls tools like getRevenueByDay, getTopSKUs, comparePeriods — running real SQL on your real database — and synthesizes answers in plain language.

No more month-old reports. No more 'I'll get back to you next week.' Ask 'which 3 products lost margin in Q4?' and get an answer in 8 seconds, with the underlying numbers shown.
